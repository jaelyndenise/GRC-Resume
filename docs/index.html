The path here

The Ascent

Not a résumé — a climb. Each stop taught the next.

I · Origin — before the code

Before security, there was a classroom. I taught Algebra II to teenagers at a recovery high school and ran top-rated technical support for Apple — years of making something complicated make sense to someone who needed it, fast. Everything I do now still rests on that.

II · The forging — learning to build

I learned to build things that don't fall over. Full-stack work in React, Node, and PostgreSQL — 200+ tickets resolved, CI/CD pipelines and automated tests that cut regression defects by 70%. This is where I stopped fearing the source code and started reading it.

III · Trial by fire — breaking things on purpose

Then I learned to take them apart. As a web-application penetration tester I went after high-profile targets with BurpSuite, NMAP, and OSINT, then wrote the reports that told teams exactly what to fix first. You defend systems better once you've been the one attacking them.

IV · The counsel — reading risk like weather

I moved from finding single holes to mapping whole climates of risk. Running assessments against SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, and GDPR, I turned tangled controls into executive-ready reports and lifted leadership's risk visibility by about 40%.

V · The current reign — the whole program

Now I hold the whole thing. As sole security engineer I built a program from zero to SOC 2 Type II, cut critical vulnerabilities by 63% in six months, and reduced IAM provisioning errors by 40% — all while still shipping full-stack features to the product. Auditor and engineer, in one person.

The Work

Three rooms, three problems. Each one its own world.
IThe Cloud Vault

CloudSaw

Local-first AWS security scanning

Problem Cloud security tools ship your findings — ARNs, account IDs, resource names — off to someone else's servers. For a lot of teams, that alone is a dealbreaker.

Build A desktop app that runs entirely on your machine: it uses your existing AWS credentials, provisions a least-privilege scanner role via bundled Terraform, scans with ScoutSuite, and maps every finding to the control it breaks.

Impact Full cloud-posture visibility with zero data exfiltration — nothing ever leaves your machine.

IIThe Seeker's Atlas

Remo

AI-assisted job search

Problem Job hunting is death by a thousand browser tabs — the same forms and screeners retyped a hundred times, with the best-fit roles buried under the newest ones.

Build A platform that aggregates listings from job boards and ATS systems, scores every role against your preferences instead of its post date, auto-fills applications through a browser extension, and tailors your resume per role.

Impact Less tab-juggling, more interviews — the whole pipeline, from discovery to applied, on one board.
